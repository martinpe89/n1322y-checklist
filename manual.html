N1322YT182T Checklist App · How to use it

checklist.deep.com.co

01 First time · once per device

  • Open the invite link from the partners’ chat — the device connects by itself.
  • Tap Install on the banner (iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen). Full screen, works with no signal.
  • Set your 4-digit signature PIN when asked — it signs your flight close-outs.

    • 02 Before engine start

  • Select your name → Begin preflight.
  • Tap every line as you complete it. Each checklist announces when it is complete.
  • Tachometer card: photograph the Garmin ENG HRS and the TOTAL HOURS drum. Readings fill in automatically — verify them, enter the tenths, then check the item.

    • 03 In flight

  • No signal required. Everything is saved on the device and syncs by itself when coverage returns.
  • Status pill, bottom-right:
    • CONNECT — enter code SYNC — uploading OFFLINE — will sync later SIGNATURE PENDING — tap & sign HIDDEN — all synced

    04 After shutdown

  • Securing Airplane checklist → photograph both gauges again.
  • Close out flight → Save to logbook → sign with your PIN.
  • Your hours, photos and maintenance share are now on record for all partners.

    • 05 Hours & money

  • Logbook: hours per partner and per flight, with tachometer photo evidence. Partners: set the maintenance rate per hour. Export CSV for accounting.

    • Rules of the air

  • One open flight at a time — the server enforces it.
  • The shutdown reading can never be lower than the start reading.
  • Hours flown without a closed flight show up as a GAP. The system sees them.
  • Cockpit aid only — the approved POH on board governs.
    • N1322Y · Cessna T182T NAV III · GFC 700 AFCS Takeoff is optional. Coming home never is.
